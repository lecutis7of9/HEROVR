## Spider-Man 1 (iilyn's SpidyVR 0.7.0, Spider-Man Remastered 4.630.0.0 on Steam)

**Spider-Man 1 support is untested in-game.** It is built and checked on our side, but nobody has played it in a
headset yet. Please send reports (launcher > Reports). iilyn's graphics, performance, anti-aliasing and files are
never touched; the add-on only loads while Spider-Man 1 plays, for your Windows user.

What is in (built, not yet tried in the game):
- **Wrist hero menu** like SM2 (right hand on the left wrist, pull the right trigger), with hero select, outfits
  (names and artists) and the hero's holo card.
- **Hero powers** hit the enemy your combat has ringed, with a beam.
- **Flight** for Iron Man, Superman, Thor and Green Lantern (hold a grip to take off; the left stick steers).
- **Hero suits as new options:** the Spider-Man 1 page's **Install suits** button adds the ten heroes as **new**
  suits in the game's Suits menu, next to Spider-Man's. Nothing is replaced: the install only adds, and it refuses
  to run if it would change one of the game's own suits. **Remove suits** puts the game's files back exactly.
  Close the game first; it takes effect the next time the game starts. (The Overstrike / ALERT way, by Tkachov.)
- **Wrist holo and suits:** picking a hero on the wrist tells you to pick that hero's suit in the game's **Suits
  menu** (or to install the suits in the launcher first). It does not switch the suit live yet: there is no
  confirmed way to change the suit from inside Spider-Man 1 4.630.0.0, so we do not guess.

What is not in yet:
- **Held props** (Thor's hammer, Captain America's shield, claws and other weapons in your hands).
- **Grapples** (the heroes' own grapples; Spider-Man's web swinging is the game's and iilyn's, unchanged).
- **Vehicles** (motorcycle, van, Batmobiles).
- A live suit switch from the wrist holo (use the game's Suits menu).
