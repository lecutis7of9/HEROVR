# HERO VR 0.0.75 - Made by Agent Mulder

> Work in progress: these notes grow as 0.0.75 is built and tested.

## What's new
- **MOTORCYCLE for CAPTAIN AMERICA and WOLVERINE (on by default):** tap **X** (a short press; holding X is still
  the abilities' modifier) and a motorcycle arrives where you look, like the Punisher van and the Batmobile. Grip
  the **left handlebar** to get on, grip both bars and **turn them** to steer, ride with the triggers as in the van
  (one hand on: its trigger rides, its trigger + stick back reverses; both hands: RT ride, LT reverse, both brake),
  and grip **beside the seat's left** to get off. Its small screen over the bars has MUSIC, SIT and GET OUT. It is
  **driver only** (no passenger seat). If its model is missing you get a note on the wrist ("MODEL MISSING, no bike")
  and nothing else changes. While CAP or WOLVERINE is played, X no longer opens the game's back/menu in gameplay
  (the same as BATMAN and PUNISHER).
- **SPIDER-MAN 1 in the launcher:** two big options at the top, **SPIDER-MAN 2** (HERO VR, unchanged) and
  **SPIDER-MAN 1** (*iilyn's Spider-Man VR mod, with Mulder's Heroes add-on*). Spider-Man 1 downloads iilyn's mod
  (Spidy by Ilya Mezerowsky, GPL-3.0) from his own GitHub, unchanged, and opens his launcher with all of his graphics,
  performance and AA settings. Anything missing gets one "No / Yes, Autosetup" pop-up. The server list shows only the
  picked game's games. Mulder's Heroes add-on (heroes, wrist menu, flight) is separate files; its in-game parts are
  CANDIDATE and need in-game testing (docs/SM1-ADDON-0.0.75.md).
