# HERO VR 0.0.77

**EXPERIMENTAL BUILD: 0.0.77 may crash or have issues. 0.0.63 is the most recent STABLE build** (Revert > USE 0.0.75,
USE 0.0.74 or USE 0.0.63 goes back, settings kept).

**0.0.77 is now available in the launcher.** Press **Check for update** (or accept the update prompt) and the launcher
updates you to 0.0.77. Version 0.0.77 (build Update 39.10), launcher build 19 (0.0.77.1).

- **Cutscenes: flat, 3D or immersive.** live.txt `cutscenes`: `flat` (the default, the game's frame on the virtual
  screen as before), `3d` (the game camera's stereo pair on the virtual screen) or `immersive` (you stand in the
  scene, the head levelled at its start, subtitles and QTE prompts on a panel). A pause or a menu always goes to the
  normal VR screen.
- **Story moves and the VR controls cheat sheet.** Hold VR X for the game's abilities (punch, Y / right stick up, B,
  A, right stick click for the ultimate, right stick down for camo); LB + RB throws aim where you look (or point);
  B dodges everywhere, a quick sideways head step dodges, both controllers buzz at spider-sense's NOW. The phone's
  STORY MOVES card is the cheat sheet of the VR controls.
- **Sandman.** A hand whose web held now punches when an enemy or the boss is near, and the boss fight's debris
  throws are logged with his HP (THROW WATCH).
- **Boss damage.** The boss's HP is read for every hero (also from classes derived from the game's BotHealth), so
  BOSS DAMAGE shows real HP.
- **Flat main menu.** The title, the front end and the pause menus are always the flat VR screen; immersive is only
  for in-game scenes.
- **Wrist minimap (in progress).** Left hand to the right wrist + left trigger shows the game's own HUD minimap over
  that wrist; still being worked on.
- **Ledge fix.** A, the web gesture or a web that holds now leaves the game's floor crawl (ledges, roof edges), so you
  are no longer stuck on a ledge after a scene.
- **Known issues:** the game's **Y zip prompt** and the **objective waypoint** do not show in VR yet.
- Spider-Man 2 only in this build (the Spider-Man 1 option is paused).
- New install: download `HERO-VR-Launcher-0.0.77.zip`, extract it, double-click `HERO VR Launcher.cmd`.
