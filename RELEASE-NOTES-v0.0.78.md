# HERO VR 0.0.78

**EXPERIMENTAL BUILD: 0.0.78 may crash or have issues. 0.0.63 is the most recent STABLE build** (Revert > USE 0.0.75,
USE 0.0.74 or USE 0.0.63 goes back, settings kept).

**0.0.78 is now available in the launcher.** Press **Check for update** (or accept the update prompt) and the launcher
updates you to 0.0.78. Version 0.0.78 (build Update 39.11), launcher build 21 (0.0.78.1).

## What's new

- **Immersive cutscenes on by default.** The game's scenes play around you. New launcher setting **CUTSCENES**
  (Comfort): Flat / 3D / Immersive, default Immersive. An old live.txt cutscenes = flat moves to immersive once.
- **Title, menus and pause stay flat.** The title, its menus and the pause menu are always the flat VR screen, also
  after loading a save from the title (scenes no longer get stuck flat after a load).
- **Pause resumes immersive.** Pause during an immersive scene (menu button, or both thumbsticks held in under
  SteamVR) and the scene goes back to immersive when you close the pause menu.
- **Game prompts as small VR cards (candidate).** The game's own button prompts, gadget wheel, waypoint and markers
  show as small cards where the game draws them, never a full 2D screen in gameplay (live.txt prompt_layer = off).
- **Sandman.** The Y card shows at his phase gate (233.3 of 250 HP) and Y goes to the game as pressed in the boss
  fight. Tip: throw his debris back (LB + RB) until he is low, then press Y when it shows.
- **Punisher: pistol and pump only.** The suppressed pistol (right leg) and the pump shotgun (back) with the old
  Punisher suit; the sniper and rifle are off for now.
- **Quiet launcher log.** The launcher's log box no longer beeps (the Windows alert sound) while reports and logs
  are written.
- **Also:** the title flat from the first frame, no ghost 2D screen in immersive scenes, vehicle seats nearer the
  wheel and bars, levelled hero weapon sounds, a What's New list in the launcher. Spider-Man 2 only (SM1 paused).

## Known bugs

- **Known bug: Sandman occasionally doesn't progress.** Being investigated.
- **Known bug: Peter's clean-up-the-house objective** can be buggy.
- **Known bug: Mary Jane's tunnel chase** can be buggy.
- **Known bug: the UI can follow your eyes** instead of staying in its place in the world. Tip: keep the UI hidden unless you
  are scanning.
- **Known bug: gliding can be hard.** Tip: use a flying hero for those parts if needed.
