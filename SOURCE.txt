HERO VR 0.0.7: source code (GNU GPL version 3)

This zip contains the complete corresponding source code:
- the VR mod is in the folder's top level (src\, tools\, CMakeLists.txt, tests\ ...). It is built by tools\build.ps1 -Observer;
- the HERO VR launcher is in tools\launcher\ (src\*.cs, assets\, build.ps1). It is built by tools\launcher\build.ps1;
- third-party libraries are fetched at their pinned revisions by tools\bootstrap.ps1 (see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
