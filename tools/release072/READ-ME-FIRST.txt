HERO VR 0.0.72 - PRIVATE TEST BUILD (not a release; do not share)
Made by Agent Mulder. Model credits: THIRD-PARTY-MODELS.md (in this zip).

INSTALL (over your 0.0.70)
1. Close the game and the HERO VR launcher.
2. Back up this folder first (copy it somewhere):
      C:\Users\candl\Downloads\HERO-VR-Launcher-0.0.70\HERO-VR-0.0.70
3. Extract this zip INTO that folder, so that "Launch Spidy VR.cmd" and the new
   "Models" and "Sounds" folders sit side by side. Say YES to replace the files.
   (build\windows-ninja\*.dll are replaced; your live.txt settings are kept.)
4. Start the launcher as usual. The launcher still says 0.0.70; the in-headset
   test notice (after the body calibration) says this is the 0.0.72 test build.
5. To undo: put your backup folder back (or use the launcher's Revert).
SHA256SUMS.txt lists every file's hash, so you can check the download.

WHERE THE SETTINGS ARE
Left wrist HUD > COMBAT page > HERO PROFILES. Only one hero can be on at a time.
Every setting has a one-line note saying whether it needs a restart (none do,
except LENS FLARES, which applies when VR next starts).

TEST UNLOCKS (for testing only)
COMBAT > TEST UNLOCKS: SUITS opens every suit, ULTIMATES opens ultimates and
suits, ALL also opens every perk. OFF puts your real progress back.

WHAT TO TRY, HERO BY HERO
Rule everywhere: the triggers fire only basic projectiles; everything else is a
gesture, grip or face button. Nothing appears from nowhere: you reach for it
and grip, and everything starts holstered or sheathed.

- SPIDER-MAN: unchanged (vanilla).
- IRON MAN: every trigger pull fires a repulsor. Flight is on by default.
- DAREDEVIL: batons start on your back; reach over a shoulder and grip to draw.
  BATON GRAPPLE: cast a baton arm up fast to grapple there.
- BATMAN: batarangs from the belt (reach + grip), grapple gun, glide, smoke.
  GRAPPLE: aim at a ledge and pull the trigger; the line flies out, then
  pulls you up (from the ground too). The gun hangs on your belt. Your game
  body is hidden as Batman (no Iron Spider), and so are the game's hands.
  Batarangs sit in your fingers.
  PERCH TAKEDOWN is COMING SOON (its game hooks are not confirmed yet).
- WOLVERINE: squeeze the grip into a fist to pop the claws (SNIKT); let go to
  sheathe them.
- SUPERMAN: flight on by default; heat vision (two thin beams from your eyes,
  sparks, scorch, a smooth looping sound); cold breath.
- PUNISHER: pistols in thigh holsters, sawed-off on your back, drawn with
  grip. Muzzle flash, hit puffs, enemies flinch; the ammo counter stays.
  Your own sounds play for the shotgun, pistols and the pistol reload.
  GRAPPLE HOOK: draw from the chest, push forward to grapple up.
  PUNISHER VAN (EXPERIMENTAL, OFF by default): tap X to summon the black
  GMC Vandura. Grip the driver door handle to get in and out, grip the
  steering wheel rim with both hands to turn it, RT drives, LT reverses,
  both triggers brake. TEST: the van does not carry you through the city
  yet (engine preview); getting in brings the van to you.
- CAPTAIN AMERICA: the shield is on your back (the real model); reach + grip.
  A thrown shield flies back and eases into your hand. The aim marker is
  small and faint.
- THOR: lightning with your own two sounds. Mjolnir starts on your back:
  reach over your right shoulder and grip to draw it. COMBAT > THOR HAMMER
  picks the model: BOOOOP (default), DEVILSEYE, LOVE THUNDER, FCCOOPER.
- GREEN LANTERN: ring constructs (fist, beam, bubble, chain), ring flight.
- COMING SOON suits are labelled COMING SOON in the suit list.
- ENEMY FACTION: the Hand, Hydra and the Joker Gang stay the game's own enemies
  in this build (the enemy swap hook is not confirmed yet).

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
A missing or broken model shows MODEL MISSING and the feature turns itself off;
the game keeps running in VR. COMBAT > USE MY OWN OR GAME SUIT is the fallback
for a suit glitch. With a report, please send the newest files in the mod
folder's "reports" subfolder.
