HERO VR 0.0.73 - PRIVATE TEST BUILD 1 (commit 7e8ef60; not a release; do not share)
Made by Agent Mulder. Model credits: payload\THIRD-PARTY-MODELS.md.

INSTALL (2 steps - no copying by hand)
1. Extract ALL 4 part zips (part1-install, part2-models, part3-models,
   part4-models) to the SAME place, e.g. your Downloads folder:
   right-click > Extract All > Extract. You get ONE folder,
   HERO-VR-0.0.73-test1, with INSTALL-TEST-BUILD.bat in it.
2. Close the game and the launcher, then double-click INSTALL-TEST-BUILD.bat.
   It checks the download, lists your HERO VR mod folders (newest play first,
   backup copies marked) and installs into the one you pick (Enter = [1]).
   Every file it replaces is kept in that folder's _before-0.0.73-test.
   Your settings (live.txt) and reports are not touched.

HOW YOU KNOW THE TEST BUILD IS RUNNING
- Launcher console, first line: "HERO VR 0.0.73 test 7e8ef60 (test build 1) - mod folder ..."
- Wrist HUD bottom line: "TEST 1 7e8ef60".
- The TEST NOTICE pops up after calibration.

TO UNDO
Copy <mod folder>\_before-0.0.73-test back over the mod folder, or use the
launcher's Revert. Every new setting can be turned back on the wrist HUD.

WHAT IS NEW IN TEST 1
- PUNISHER VAN (EXPERIMENTAL, OFF by default; COMBAT > HERO PROFILES >
  PUNISHER): tap X to summon it, grip the driver door handle to get in. You
  now really drive: the van carries your body through the city. Hold the
  wheel rim to steer, RT throttle, LT reverse, both triggers brake. You are
  bulletproof inside. Grip the inside door handle (at a crawl) to get out;
  you are walked out beside the driver door. If driving is not safe the van
  parks where it is (the old parked van) - never a crash.
  Sound: door slam + start-up once, then the engine loop (pitch and volume
  follow the throttle); getting out fades the engine, then the door slams.
- UNIVERSAL GRAPPLE: grip draws the grapple device, THEN the trigger fires.
  A trigger pressed on the hologram/menus never fires it.
  BATMAN: grip away from the pouches draws the grapple gun.
  CAPTAIN AMERICA: has a grapple gun in the hand that is NOT the shield arm.
  PUNISHER: the chest hook has its own zone (higher, narrower than the
  shotgun's); grip at the chest draws it, trigger fires. No push gesture.
  DAREDEVIL: tap the trigger to throw a baton, HOLD it to grapple along the
  baton (from its tip).
  Grappling an ENEMY reels you to about 2 ft of him.
- CAPTAIN AMERICA: the thrown shield comes back to the forearm it left;
  it goes on your back only when you reach back and grip. SUPER SPRINT:
  pump your arms with the left stick pushed (COMBAT > CAP SPRINT).
- DAREDEVIL: batons the right way round; a thrown baton has no rope - it
  flies free and comes back.
- Thrown props bounce off buildings, the floor and cars.
- Holsters turn with your body.
- WOLVERINE: the Jacket-and-Jeans suit's real claws are now the claws of
  EVERY Wolverine suit, sliding out with the grip (never double claws).
- Only SPIDER-MAN climbs walls. Wolverine CLAW CLIMB is OFF by default.
- No web lines for heroes other than Spider-Man.
- IRON MAN: the palm turns to the aim and blasts leave the palm, with a
  visible repulsor beam.
- MIRROR: shows your held and holstered props too.
- Roster page flicker fix.
- SMOOTH TURN is the default (set SMOOTH TURN to OFF for snap turning).

KNOWN LIMITS IN TEST 1

- Spider-Man in the mirror is still the stylised figure (no real model).

- Coming in TEST 2: far window flicker, street light flicker, desktop

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
A missing or broken model shows MODEL MISSING and the feature turns itself
off; the game keeps running in VR. Send the newest files in the mod
folder's "reports" subfolder.
